CC BY-SA 4.0. OPRA-ATTRIBUTION.txt records what was adapted and how.

AutoEq provides the impulse responses behind the convolution layer, and is the
origin of much of the data OPRA redistributes.

LAME, through @breezystack/lamejs (LGPL-3.0)
--------------------------------------------
The Karaoke Maker writes an MP3 beside the WAV when it splits a song. Chromium
decodes MP3 and cannot encode it, so that encoder is LAME — the LAME project's
encoder, ported to JavaScript as @breezystack/lamejs 1.2.7.

FluidEQ acknowledges its use of LAME. The LAME project's own site is
https://lame.sourceforge.net, and the port is published at
https://github.com/breezystack/lamejs.
