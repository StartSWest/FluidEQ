# Scene publishing preview — release-note draft

Draft for a future release; local implementation and review are still in progress.

- **Watch your scene before publishing.** Studio prepares a silent ten-second video in the Publish screen. Watch the clip there, record it again, or cancel before sending your scene for review.
- **Your cover stays your choice.** Recording a video keeps the cover you selected. A change to the scene or its settings needs a fresh clip, so the preview matches the work you publish.
- The video carries a small **FluidEQ Plus · Preview** watermark and records the scene itself.

This draft does not add a What's New item or tour step.
